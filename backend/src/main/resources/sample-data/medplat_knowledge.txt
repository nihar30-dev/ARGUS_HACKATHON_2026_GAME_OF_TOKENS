MEDplat Digital Health Platform — Complete Knowledge Base

== PRODUCT OVERVIEW ==

MEDplat is an AI-powered digital health platform that connects patients, doctors, hospitals, and diagnostic centers through a unified ecosystem. It delivers end-to-end clinical workflow automation using HL7 FHIR APIs, telemedicine, AI-assisted diagnostics, and offline-first architecture optimized for India's diverse healthcare landscape.

MEDplat is purpose-built for mid-to-large hospitals, multi-specialty chains, and government health programs seeking to digitize patient journeys from pre-registration through post-discharge follow-up. The platform replaces fragmented point solutions with a single integrated system, reducing administrative burden, diagnostic errors, and patient dropout rates.

Key product lines include: Hospital Information System (HIS), Electronic Health Records (EHR), Telemedicine, AI Diagnostics, Pharmacy Management, Laboratory Information System (LIS), Patient App, and Doctor App.

== CORE CAPABILITIES ==

Appointment and Queue Management: Patients book appointments via web, app, or walk-in kiosk. Smart queuing reduces average waiting time by 35–45%. OPD tokens are managed digitally and displayed on department screens.

Electronic Health Records (EHR): Longitudinal patient records with structured clinical notes, vitals tracking, allergy alerts, and medication history. Records are accessible across all departments in real time. The EHR is HL7 FHIR R4 compliant, enabling interoperability with national and state health registries.

Telemedicine: Built-in video consultation module supports synchronous and asynchronous consultations. Doctors can prescribe, refer, and order diagnostics within the teleconsultation interface. Supports over 1,000 concurrent sessions with low-latency video infrastructure.

AI-Assisted Diagnostics: Machine learning models for radiology interpretation (chest X-ray, CT, MRI), pathology slide analysis, and clinical risk scoring. AI flags abnormal reports and prioritizes critical cases for radiologist review, reducing turnaround time by up to 60%.

Pharmacy Management: Integrated drug dispensing with expiry tracking, stock alerts, and automated billing. Supports both in-patient and outpatient prescriptions. NABH-compliant label printing and batch tracking.

Laboratory Information System (LIS): Sample tracking from collection to result reporting. Supports NABL-accredited labs with QC workflows. Results auto-populate into patient EHR and trigger alerts for critical values.

== OFFLINE-FIRST ARCHITECTURE ==

MEDplat uses an offline-first design that keeps clinical operations running without internet connectivity. Local edge devices synchronize patient data in real time when online and queue writes offline for seamless sync when connectivity restores.

This architecture is critical for tier-2 and tier-3 cities, primary health centers, and community hospitals where internet reliability is inconsistent. Offline capabilities cover: registration, vitals recording, prescription writing, lab result entry, and billing.

Data sync uses conflict-resolution logic with timestamp precedence and clinician override to prevent data loss during synchronization.

== ABDM AND INTEROPERABILITY ==

MEDplat is ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission) compliant, supporting ABHA ID creation, health record linking, and health locker integration. Patients can share their health records across hospitals using their ABHA number without paper-based referrals.

FHIR R4 APIs allow integration with state health management information systems (HMIS), insurance portals, and third-party diagnostic labs. MEDplat supports SNOMED CT, ICD-10, LOINC, and RxNorm terminologies out of the box.

Integration timeline: standard ABDM onboarding takes 4–6 weeks; custom API integrations for complex HIS replacements take 8–12 weeks with dedicated implementation support.

== ROLE-BASED ACCESS AND SECURITY ==

MEDplat enforces granular role-based access control (RBAC) with over 80 permission nodes. Common roles include: Super Admin, Hospital Admin, Doctor, Nurse, Pharmacist, Lab Technician, Receptionist, Billing Executive, and Patient.

Data security features: AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, audit logs for all data access events, IP whitelisting, multi-factor authentication, and session timeout controls.

The platform is SOC 2 Type II certified, HIPAA-aligned, and undergoes annual penetration testing. Patient consent management follows DPDP (Digital Personal Data Protection) Act 2023 requirements.

Close: Propose a 45-minute discovery workshop with the CIO and CMO to map current workflows. This creates internal champions before a formal proposal. Ask for the next meeting within 10 business days.
